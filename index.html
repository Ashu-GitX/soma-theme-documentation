01 / START

First setup

  1. Upload the SŌMA ZIP in Online Store → Themes.
  2. Preview the theme and open the theme editor.
  3. Add your logo, main menu, favicon, colors, and Shopify-hosted fonts.
  4. Connect homepage sections to products and collections.
  5. Test search, cart, customer accounts, policies, and checkout before publishing.
SŌMA uses your Shopify store name when no custom wordmark is supplied. Empty media fields show neutral Shopify placeholders and never import demo products or imagery.
02 / COMPOSE

Homepage sections

Cinematic hero

Add up to three image or hosted-video slides. A slide can use uploaded media, a selected product, or the matching product from the fallback collection. Set the autoplay interval from 3–12 seconds.

Collections and categories

Select live Shopify collections. Titles, prices, availability, links, and product media update from the catalog automatically.

Shop the look and social video

Connect hotspots and video cards to real products. Upload only media you own or are licensed to publish.

Partners and testimonials

These sections are disabled on first install. Enable them only after adding genuine testimonials and approved brand assets.

03 / FIND

Navigation and search

Choose the main menu inside the Header section. The desktop Shop control and mobile half-sheet use collection cards configured in the same section.

Predictive search covers product titles, product types, vendors, variant titles, and variant SKUs. Products must be active and available to the Online Store channel.

04 / SELL

Product pages

Product media comes directly from Shopify. Add multiple images, hosted videos, or 3D models to enable the swipeable gallery and thumbnails. Variant price and availability update using Shopify product data.

The pairing module uses Shopify product recommendations. Configure complementary products through Shopify Search & Discovery when available.

05 / TUNE

Appearance and motion

Theme settings control the light and dark palettes, display and body fonts, page width, and motion. SŌMA respects the visitor’s reduced-motion preference. Check text contrast after changing colors.

06 / SUPPORT

Support

Support covers installation, documented theme settings, reproducible theme defects, current Shopify storefront compatibility, and guidance for built-in sections.

Support excludes custom development, third-party app debugging, content entry, and recovery of modified code. Initial responses are provided within two business days.

Troubleshooting

  • Empty section: select a product or collection and confirm Online Store availability.
  • Missing SKU result: confirm the SKU is saved on an active product variant.
  • Missing recommendations: configure complementary products or allow Shopify to generate related products.
  • Video not playing: use Shopify-hosted video in the section or product media.
